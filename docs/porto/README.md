## PORTO TARGET WITH DECREASING GAIN

This estimate uses these assumptions:

- The monthly gain decreases linearly from `100%` at `$200` of capital to
  `12%` at `$300,000` of capital.
- Capital in the system cannot exceed `$300,000`.
- After the portfolio reaches the system limit, the gain stays at `12%` of
  `$300,000`. The portfolio therefore gains a fixed `$36,000` per month.
- `Gain (%)` is the return on **Capital in System**, not on the total portfolio.

Before the system limit, the monthly gain percentage is calculated as:

```text
Gain = 100% - ((Capital in System - $200) / ($300,000 - $200) * 88%)
```
